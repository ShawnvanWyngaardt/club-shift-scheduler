# v0.2.0: from a working loop to something a club could try

v0.1.0 proved the loop: members say when they can help, the solver shares out the shifts, outcomes feed the points. v0.2.0 makes it usable without me in the room.

## For visitors

- **Try the demo:** every visitor gets a private sandbox club, removed after 24 hours.
- **Start your own club** from the landing page.

## For coordinators

- **Setup screen:** add members, shift types and weekly repeating shifts, and remove what isn't used yet.
- **Refill open slots** after publishing, without touching anyone already scheduled.
- **Remove or reassign** someone on an upcoming shift. Their answer is cleared, so a refill doesn't hand the shift straight back.
- **Points tab:** who is next in line, and the history behind every balance.

## For members

Changes driven by the first user test:

- **My answers:** clear that ticks are your answers, not a schedule.
- **My shifts:** what you were given and why, past outcomes, and how you earned your points.
- **Club points:** everyone's points alphabetically, so you can see the work is shared.
- Past shifts are hidden; a broken link shows a clear page with what to do next.

## Under the hood

- Database migrations with Alembic, checked in CI
- API split into routers, with shared access checks
- Ruff, oxlint and ESLint in CI

## Known limitations

See the README. The first fix after this release: club data readable without a token.
